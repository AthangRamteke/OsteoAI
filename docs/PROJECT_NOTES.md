# OsteoAI - Project Notes

## Project Overview

OsteoAI is an AI-powered Osteoporosis Risk Assessment System developed as a Final Year Project.

The goal of OsteoAI is to identify individuals who may be at risk of developing osteoporosis before serious complications such as fractures occur.

Unlike traditional BMI calculators or basic health assessment tools, OsteoAI combines multiple health factors with Machine Learning to provide a personalized osteoporosis risk prediction and preventive recommendations.

---

# Problem Statement

Osteoporosis is known as a "silent disease" because it usually develops without noticeable symptoms until a fracture occurs.

Many people ignore important lifestyle and medical risk factors due to a lack of awareness and accessible screening tools.

Existing online tools mostly provide general health advice or BMI calculations rather than predicting osteoporosis risk using Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Project Objectives

The main objectives of OsteoAI are:

- Assess personal health information
- Assess lifestyle habits
- Assess medical history
- Predict osteoporosis risk using Machine Learning
- Explain prediction using Explainable AI (SHAP)
- Provide personalized health recommendations
- Encourage preventive healthcare
